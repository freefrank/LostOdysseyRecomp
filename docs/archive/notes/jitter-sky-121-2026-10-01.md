Issue #121 reports a flickering sky at Old Sorceress' Mansion on v0.7.25 (D3D12, DLSS DLAA, frame generation 2×, 120 FPS; disabling the wide screen and the 120 FPS cap did not help). Both attached runtime logs contain the same runtime suspect line right after the map `ge1_0_scrw` loads:

| Log | Frame | Sky draw | Depth companion |
|---|---|---|---|
| `runtime.log` | 1726 | 366 | `b030ab4e17a20783`, draw 93 |
| `runtime-1790885896209923.log` | 70679 | 310 | `b030ab4e17a20783`, draw 104 |

Both are `depth_writer_after_jittered_geometry` for VS `bda41a11626a545c` with PS `02ee5f0608be581a`, camera at c7–c10, depth write on. The VS is the #67 Grand Staff sky VS and the PS is the #102 Legacy of the Eastern Tribe sky PS. `SkyMaterialPairs` listed only the exact pairs `bda41`+`a9e9` and `db23`+`02ee`, and the f3449 test even asserted that `bda41`+`02ee` stays unmapped, so this sky drew unjittered over the jittered `b030` depth: the #67/#102 failure in a third combination.

## Change

`bda41`+`02ee` is added to `SkyMaterialPairs` with the whole-frame motion fallback of #67, because the VS is the same. Like the other sky pairs it jitters only against a scene camera observed earlier in the frame with bit-identical constants, and never anchors the frame itself. `PositionVPSlot` still excludes `bda41` as a whole.
