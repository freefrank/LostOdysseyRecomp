In a Vulkan session at 3840x2160 with DLSS DLAA (RTX 5080, source version 0.7.25), the runtime suspect log reported one unmapped depth writer right after the map `gp5_0_scrw` (Ice Canyon - Snowy Plateau) loaded:

`kind=depth_writer_after_jittered_geometry vs=bda41a11626a545c ps=1dee52ba32155a53 frame=11525 draw=35 camera_slot=7 evidence_slot=7 zfunc=6 zwrite=true companion_vs=b030ab4e17a20783 companion_slot=4 companion_draw=12 same_world=true same_camera=true`

The VS is the #67/#121 sky VS. `SkyMaterialPairs` maps exact pairs only, and PS `1dee` had never been reviewed with it, so this sky drew unjittered over the jittered `b030` depth: the #67/#102/#121 failure with another PS. The 2026-09-30 feedback archive lists seven PS partners of `bda41` (`e086`, `a9e9`, `fd46`, `72bc`, `1693`, `e2b8`, `02ee`) and no `1dee`.

The user then pressed F1 there: `render-17909028898149728-f12139` (one of three frames complete; the ZIP step failed, so it is a folder). Its `runtime.log` is the same session log and places the capture at `gp5_0_scrw`.

Shader review, from `shaders/<hash>.hlsl` in the capture:

- VS `bda41` is the program reviewed for #67 and #121: world c0–c3 (lines 427–434), then c7–c10 (435–442) into `oPos` (444) and its copy `o1` (469–470). c7–c10 are read nowhere else. `o0` is the UV, `o2` a tangent-space view vector from the c11 eye and c4–c6, `o3` the c6 vector in tangent space, `o4` the vertex color.
- PS `1dee` reads `i1` only as `i1.w`, which it writes to `oC0.w` (lines 460–461); `r1` is overwritten (464–477) before any other read. It never reads `SV_Position`. tex1 (line 432) is sampled at the `i0` UV transformed by c0, c4, c5, c254 and c255; tex0 (452, 454) at that UV distorted by the tex1 result. Both are static guest textures (DXT1 512x512 at 0x65f3000, DXT4/5 512x512 at 0x6623000), not resolves. `tools/shader_analysis/audit_ps.py --clip-input 1` finds only the W read.
- Jitter changes only the x and y components of c7–c10, so clip Z/W, the `o1.w` the PS writes to alpha and every texture coordinate stay the same.

## Change

`bda41`+`1dee` is added to `SkyMaterialPairs` with the #67 motion fallback, as for #121, because the VS is the same. Like the other sky pairs it jitters only against a scene camera observed earlier in the frame with bit-identical constants, and never anchors the frame itself. The five other partners (`e086`, `fd46`, `72bc`, `1693`, `e2b8`) stay unmapped until their own PS review.
