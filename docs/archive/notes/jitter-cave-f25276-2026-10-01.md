In the same session as the [Ice Canyon sky](jitter-sky-f12139-2026-10-01.md) (Vulkan, 3840x2160, DLSS DLAA), the user reported a flicker in Burning Cave. The runtime suspect log has one line for it, written at the end of `gs5_0_scrw` (Burning Cave - Underground Lake), 75 ms before `gs4_0_scrw` (Strange Rocks) loaded:

`kind=depth_writer vs=61bc9947f1e88573 ps=042ef823a22e1498 frame=22612 draw=949 camera_slot=7 evidence_slot=7 zfunc=6 zwrite=true index_base=0x00275400 index_count=1590 companion_vs=0000000000000000`

A pair is logged once, at its first qualifying draw. On that transition frame the scene camera itself was not finite: every x and y component of the logged c7–c10 is ±inf or NaN, so no draw with that camera can be jittered, and the banks cannot serve as a fixture. The private 2026-09-30 flicker sweep had already listed `61bc` as a source-only VS (no telemetry) with the position chain of mapped VS, held until a capture showed its camera, depth pass and PS.

The user's F1 capture `render-17909030149370909-f25276.zip` (three complete frames) was taken at Strange Rocks after `gs4_0_scrw` loaded.

## Implementation

`PositionVPSlot` maps `61bc9947f1e88573` to slot 7 (`temporal_scene.h`). The mapping is VS-wide like the f6814 entries, so a new PS partner reopens this review. The usual runtime guards still apply (scene viewport and VTE, matching camera bits and depth allocation, finite constants), so a frame like the logged transition, whose camera is not finite, stays unjittered. As a mapped depth writer the draw now also takes part in object-motion replay instead of forcing the whole-frame motion fallback.
