# Sea of Baus battle sky jitter (#203) — 2026-10-04

Issue #203 reports low-contrast sky flicker when a battle starts in the Sea of Baus (Windows, AMD 7900 XTX, D3D12, FSR native AA at 4K). The attached logs from v0.8.10 and v0.8.15 each contain one runtime suspect line, in the frame the battle starts:

| Log | Entered from | Frame | Sky draw | Depth companion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `runtime-1791099578645265.log` (v0.8.10) | Reef Shallows (`ua3_0_scrw`) | 11850 | 250 | `b030ab4e17a20783`, draw 23 |
| `runtime-1791121486142211.log` (v0.8.15) | Beach of the Wastes (`ua4_0_scrw`) | 111083 | 250 | `b030ab4e17a20783`, draw 22 |

Both are `depth_writer_after_jittered_geometry` for VS `bda41a11626a545c` with PS `fd46e0190f5f6c50`, camera at c7–c10, depth write on, same world and camera as the depth draw. The VS is the #67 sky VS. The 2026-09-25 feedback review had listed `fd46` as an observed PS of this VS without runtime evidence. The suspect is present in v0.8.10, so 199ad1e (v0.8.15) did not cause it.

`tools/capture_analysis/triage_suspect.py` against the HLSL dump of the known shaders: `oPos` uses only the slot-7 matrix, the PS reads only `i1.w`, and both texture fetches use mesh coordinates (`i0`). The only branches are the translator's alpha test and debug views. This is the same class as the six sky PS of the 2026-10-01 map tour.

## Change

`bda41`+`fd46` is added to `SkyMaterialPairs` with the #67 motion fallback.
